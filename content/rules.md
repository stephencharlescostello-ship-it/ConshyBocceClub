# Table Shuffleboard Rules

## The basics
- Two teams play on the long table. A **match** is best of three **games**; a game is played to **15 points** (no win-by-two) unless the season format says otherwise.
- Each **frame**, both sides slide four weights from the same end, alternating shots. The team that shot second in the previous frame shoots first in the next.
- Only the side with the **farthest weight** scores in a frame. That side scores every one of its weights that lies beyond the opponent's farthest weight.
- A weight must completely cross the foul line to stay on the board. Weights that fall off or sit short of the foul line are removed.

## Scoring zones
| Where the weight rests | Points |
| --- | --- |
| In the 1 zone | 1 |
| In the 2 zone | 2 |
| In the 3 zone | 3 |
| Hanging over the end of the board (a **hanger**) | 4 |

A weight that touches a line counts for the lower zone. A hanger must overhang the end of the board; a straight-edge check settles close calls.

## Doubles and lineups
- Teams usually play doubles: partners stand at opposite ends and alternate frames. Singles games are fine when both captains agree.
- Record who played each game in the app. Lineups are informational and not enforced, but they feed the stats and badges.

## Deciding close calls
- The two captains settle disputes on the board. If they cannot agree, the frame is re-thrown.
- Use the app's **Dispute** button only for scoring entries you believe are wrong after the match.

# League Policies

## Match night
- Matches start at 6:00, 7:00 and 8:00 PM. Be at your table ten minutes early.
- Either captain, or a designated scorekeeper, runs the live scorekeeper in the app. If you keep score on the board instead, enter the final game scores with Quick Entry before you leave.
- The opposing captain confirms the result in the app. Results confirm automatically after 12 hours.

## Forfeits
- A team that cannot field at least two players 15 minutes after the start time forfeits the match. A forfeit counts as a 2–0 loss with 0 points scored.
- Tell the commissioner before 5:00 PM if you know you cannot make it so the other team can be notified.

## Subs
- Use the **Sub board** in the app. Post what you need, and available players on that night will be notified. The first player to claim the spot plays once the captain confirms.
- Subs may not play for two different teams in the same week on the same night.

## Disputes
- Only the opposing captain can dispute a submitted result, and only before it auto-confirms.
- The commissioner reviews the dispute queue and may uphold, correct, order a replay or void the match. That decision is final.

## Conduct
- Trash talk is encouraged. Slurs, threats and harassment are not, and will get you muted or removed from the league.
- Respect the club's staff, the equipment and your opponents. Clean up your table when you finish.
- Players must be 21 or older.
